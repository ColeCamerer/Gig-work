**Cedar & Smoke**
Like the last hour around a campfire — warm cedar, a curl of woodsmoke, a whisper of clove underneath. Cedar & Smoke fills a room with that settled, end-of-evening calm without ever turning heavy. Hand-poured in small batches from 100% soy wax, it burns clean and slow for 50+ hours. Cotton wick, no funny additives. Strike a match, drop into the good chair, let the day go. Perfect for fall nights, reading corners, and anyone who misses the cabin. *Notes: cedar, smoked vanilla, clove.*

**Sea Salt & Sage**
A clean ocean breath with something grounding underneath. Sea Salt & Sage opens bright and airy — cool salt air, a squeeze of citrus — then settles into soft, herbal sage that keeps it from ever smelling like a hotel lobby. Soy wax, cotton wick, 50+ hour burn, hand-poured in small batches. It's the candle for a freshly cleaned kitchen, a Monday reset, or a bathroom you want to feel like a spa. Calming without being sleepy. *Notes: sea salt, citrus, garden sage.*

**Amber & Honey**
Golden, soft, a little indulgent. Amber & Honey wraps warm amber around real honeyed sweetness and a touch of vanilla, so a room feels lit by candlelight even at noon. Never cloying — the amber keeps it grown-up. Small-batch soy wax, cotton wick, 50+ hours of slow, even burn. Light it for slow Sunday mornings, cozy company, or whenever the house needs to feel like a hug. *Notes: amber, wild honey, vanilla.*

**Fig & Black Pepper**
For people who find most candles too sweet. Fig & Black Pepper leads with ripe, green fig, then a crack of black pepper gives it edge and a grown-up, almost perfumey finish. Sophisticated, a little unexpected, never sugary. Hand-poured soy wax, cotton wick, 50+ hour burn. At home on a dinner table, a work desk, or a living room that leans modern. The one you'll keep re-buying. *Notes: fig, black pepper, light musk.*

**Lavender & Oat Milk**
The softest one we make. Lavender & Oat Milk smooths real lavender into creamy, almost edible oat milk and a touch of vanilla — calming, cozy, and not at all like the sharp lavender you're bracing for. Small-batch soy wax, cotton wick, 50+ hours. Made for wind-down: bedside tables, bath nights, the last hour before sleep. If you've never liked lavender, start here. *Notes: lavender, oat milk, vanilla.*
