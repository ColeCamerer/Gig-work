# Sample — Logo Concept Spec
[Sample — fictional client, demonstrates style. Proves the LOGO / BRAND_KIT lane.]

Client brief: specialty coffee roaster "North Bean", wants modern, warm, minimal — no cartoon cup.

## Concept
A single abstract mark: a coffee bean whose center seam doubles as a compass needle pointing north — ties the name to a clean, premium geometric symbol. Works as a stamp on bags, a tiny favicon, and a big sign.

## Generation prompt (paste into DALL·E / Midjourney / Firefly)
```
A modern minimal logo mark for a specialty coffee roaster. A single abstract
coffee bean where the center seam forms a subtle compass needle pointing up/north.
Clean geometric vector style, warm dark-brown and cream, no text, flat, high
contrast, works on white and dark backgrounds, premium and understated. No
cartoon, no realistic photo, no gradients.
```

## Colors
- Primary: `#3B2A21` — Roast Brown
- Secondary: `#EFE6D8` — Cream
- Accent: `#C2772F` — Amber

## Fonts
- Display: **Fraunces** (warm, modern serif) — wordmark
- Body: **Inter** — taglines, packaging text

## Usage
- Mark works solo (favicon, stamp) or left of the wordmark.
- Min size 32px. Keep clear space = height of the bean around it.
- Avoid: busy photo backgrounds, recoloring outside the palette.

## Recreate in Canva (if not using generated image)
1. Search "coffee bean line icon" + "compass needle" in elements.
2. Combine, recolor to Roast Brown, align the needle to the bean seam.
3. Add "North Bean" in Fraunces to the right; "specialty coffee" small in Inter.
4. Export PNG, transparent background, 1000px+.

## Alternates to offer
- B: just the compass needle inside a circle (simpler, more scalable).
- C: bean + subtle "N" negative space in the seam.
