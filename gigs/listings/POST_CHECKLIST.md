Post these by hand (these platforms ban scripted posting). Order = fastest first dollar.

- [ ] **Fiverr — Logo Design** (`fiverr/logo-design.md`) → Graphics & Design › Logo Design

## Do this week
- [ ] **Reddit [OFFER]** → r/slavelabour (different day than the r/forhire post)
- [ ] **Craigslist writing ad** (`craigslist/services-writing-design.md` → Ad 1) → services › writing (`wrs`), your metro
- [ ] **Craigslist design ad** (Ad 2) → services › creative (`crs`), your metro

## Before you post
- [ ] Fill `gigs/config.json` → identity.payment (PayPal/Venmo) and identity.reddit_username
- [ ] Have 1-2 samples ready (there's a blog sample in `gigs/samples/`) — offer them free in replies
- [ ] Set a profile photo + 1-line bio on Fiverr/Reddit (blank profiles kill conversion)

## Cadence (don't turn inbound into spam either)
- Fiverr: post once, leave up. Respond to messages fast — response time affects ranking.
- Reddit: one post per sub every 24-48h max. Follow sub rules/tags exactly.
- Craigslist: a few cities, staggered; refresh every ~48h. No links in v1.

## The first-review play
First 5 orders: price low, over-deliver, then ask for a review. Reviews move reply/conversion rate 2% → 20%. Log each in `gigs/reviews.md`. Raise prices after 3-5 reviews (note in each Fiverr listing tells you the target).
